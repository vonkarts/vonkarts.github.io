original: 'Whether a dog had actually eaten the treat made no difference to its guilty-look behaviours. Scolding did: dogs showed many more of them when they were scolded, and most of all when they were scolded without having eaten the treat.'
today: A 2012 study with 64 dogs in Hungary found that dogs that broke a rule and dogs that didn’t were equally likely to act guilty when greeted. Neither study says dogs feel nothing; they show that “the look” is not a reliable sign that a dog did something wrong.
surprise: Dogs that had been to obedience classes showed the guilty look more often when scolded.
