you_real: Both men and women kept their hand in the cold water longer while swearing, and said it hurt less.
original: Men held on for an average of 191 seconds while swearing and 147 seconds with the neutral word. Women held on for 120 and 83 seconds. Swearing also raised heart rate and lowered how painful the cold felt.
today: The effect has been repeated. In a 2020 study with 92 people, a real swear word raised pain tolerance by 33% compared with a neutral word, while invented “swear words” such as “twizpipe” did not help. A 2011 study found that people who swear a lot in everyday life get less benefit.
