today: 'In 2018, Joshua Miller and Adam Sanjurjo showed that looking at “what happened after a hit” in short sequences of shots creates a hidden bias: the method could make even a genuinely hot player look normal. Once the bias was corrected, signs of a hot hand appeared in the 1985 data. Today the debate is less about whether the effect exists and more about how big it is; most studies say it is small.'
