today: Few animals pass this test. In 2019, researchers reported that cleaner fish tried to scrape off a mark on their throat after seeing it in a mirror (3 of 4 fish), and in 2022, 17 of 18 did. Researchers still argue about whether passing the test really shows self-awareness.
