story:
- '80 patients with irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), a common gut condition with pain and bloating, were split into two groups at random. One group got no treatment. The other got a bottle labelled “placebo pills” and was told the truth: the pills were sugar pills with no active ingredient.'
- They were asked to take the pills twice a day for three weeks and told they did not have to believe in the placebo effect, only to take the pills. At the end, both groups were asked whether their symptoms had been adequately relieved.
