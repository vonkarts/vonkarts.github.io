q: What percentage of participants kept going all the way to 450 volts?
you_real: Of the real participants, 65% went to 450 volts, the very last switch.
original: 26 of the 40 men, or 65%, went all the way to the last switch. The learner was actually an actor working with the researchers and the shocks were not real, but the participants did not know that.
today: 'In 2009, Jerry Burger repeated the study within ethical limits by stopping it at 150 volts: 70% of participants were willing to go past 150 volts. Milgram’s figure at the same point was 82.5%, and the difference was not significant. On the other hand, researchers who went through Milgram’s archives found that experimenters sometimes departed from the script and that some participants suspected the shocks were fake. In Milgram’s own variations, obedience also changed a lot depending on the conditions.'
surprise: Before the study, the psychiatrists Milgram asked predicted that only about one in a thousand people would go to the last switch.
