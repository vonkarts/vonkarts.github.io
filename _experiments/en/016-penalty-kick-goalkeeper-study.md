q: In what percentage of the penalties did the goalkeeper stay in the middle?
you_real: The real goalkeepers dived in about 94% of the kicks and stayed in the middle only about 6% of the time.
original: 'Goalkeepers jumped left or right in 93.7% of the kicks. Yet staying in the middle gave them the best chance of stopping the ball: 33.3%, compared with 14.2% when they dived left and 12.6% when they dived right.'
today: 'The authors call this “action bias”: a goal conceded while standing still feels worse than one conceded while diving, so keepers prefer to be seen doing something. They also surveyed 32 top goalkeepers about their choices. If goalkeepers started staying put more often, kickers would probably change where they aim.'
surprise: Staying in the middle more than doubled the chance of a save, but it was the option goalkeepers chose least.
