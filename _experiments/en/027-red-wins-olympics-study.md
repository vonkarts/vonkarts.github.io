- In Olympic boxing, taekwondo, Greco-Roman wrestling and freestyle wrestling, each competitor wears either red or blue, and the colour is assigned at random.
- Two anthropologists at Durham University in England went through the results of these contests at the 2004 Athens Olympics and counted how often the fighter in red won.
