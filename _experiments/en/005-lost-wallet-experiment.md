original: On average, owners were contacted for 40% of wallets with no money, and 51% of wallets with money. In three countries where a larger amount (about 94 dollars) was added, the rate rose to 72%.
today: The study was published in Science in 2019 and used a very large sample. Even so, rates vary from country to country; the average does not describe behavior everywhere.
