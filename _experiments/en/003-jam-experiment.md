today: This study made “choice overload” famous around the world. But a 2010 meta-analysis combining dozens of studies found that the average effect was close to zero. Lots of options sometimes freeze people and sometimes don’t; the effect seems to appear when the decision is hard, the options are complex or time is short.
surprise: One of the most-quoted marketing experiments in the world turns out to hold only under certain conditions.
