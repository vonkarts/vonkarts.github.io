vonkarts is a personal blog. I write about landscapes, cities and the quiet in between, and about the habits and questions that come with everyday life.

On Instagram, as [@vonkarts](https://www.instagram.com/vonkarts/), I share short films of landscapes and nature.

Articles that discuss research link to the original studies so you can check the sources yourself.
